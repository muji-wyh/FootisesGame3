> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn Blaze/Ultron `214+LK` into one heavy-impact kick that launches the victim with `KB_UpperKO_Flip`.

**Architecture:** Keep the change inside Blaze's existing `MoveData`; Ultron inherits it through `Blaze.build()`. Reuse the existing authored hit-reaction override and deterministic combat harness, with no new engine fields or animation special cases.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7, typed GDScript, fixed 60 Hz simulation, Kubold animation library.

## Global Constraints

- `214+LK` connects exactly once.
- The airborne victim reaction is `KB_UpperKO_Flip`.
- Preserve the old move's total damage (`48`) and meter gain (`16`).
- Keep startup, active, recovery, hitbox, and `super_inferno` cancel unchanged.
- Do not alter global upper-knockdown animation selection.
- Ultron must inherit the same behavior without Ultron-specific code.
